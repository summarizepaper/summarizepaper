Key takeaways

- **alphaXiv is a place to read and discuss arXiv papers**: papers to explore, comments under each paper, an AI overview, audio and chat on top, and a Deep Research mode.
- **SummarizePaper is a research companion**: a complete summary page for arXiv, journal and preprint papers, an assistant whose every claim comes with the passage of the paper that supports it, and tools to follow your field, check a draft and review a research question.
- **Use alphaXiv** to see what the community says about a paper. **Use SummarizePaper** to understand a paper quickly, check what the AI says against the paper, and for the work around your reading.
- Both work from the arXiv ID, so nothing stops you from using both on the same paper.

## What alphaXiv does better

- **Discussion.** Each paper has a discussion space, so you can see questions and remarks from other readers. SummarizePaper has nothing comparable.
- **Discovery.** The home page lists papers to explore with a short summary and their view counts, lets you follow researchers and has pages for conferences such as NeurIPS. Most of what it surfaces is machine learning.
- **Tooling for developers.** alphaXiv offers a browser extension and an MCP server to plug papers into AI assistants.

## What SummarizePaper does better

- **Checking the AI.** After each answer, the assistant lists its claims with the sentence of the paper that supports each one, found word for word in the text, with its section, figure or table and a link that opens the paper on that passage. A claim the paper does not support is labelled, not hidden.
- **A complete summary on one page.** The detailed summary, key points and a lay summary are written in advance, so the page loads ready to read. See [an example](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/paper/2302.13303v1/).
- **Beyond arXiv.** Journal articles from their DOI, bioRxiv, medRxiv, HAL and other open repositories get the same summary page. The full text is read when the license allows it; otherwise the page says the summary comes from the abstract.
- **Reading outside your field.** The lay summary and blog-style article explain the paper without jargon, and any summary can be rewritten for a 12-year-old, a high school student, an undergraduate or an expert, in 16 languages.
- **Following your field.** The free [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) emails new arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv and journal papers from the fields you choose. Premium ranks them by closeness to your research, reads the digest aloud, and adds [citation alerts](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/citations/) and a [scoop watch](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/scoops/) that emails you when a new paper comes close to your project.
- **The work around the paper.** Check your draft's references and find the papers it misses (Premium), review the literature on a research question, prepare journal club slides or a press kit (Premium+). See [all the tools](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/tools/).

## Who SummarizePaper is not for

If what you value is the conversation around a paper, or a social feed of what others read, alphaXiv is the better home. SummarizePaper has no comments and no social features: it is built for understanding papers, checking what an AI says about them and the research work around them.
