Key takeaways

- **alphaXiv is a place to read and discuss arXiv papers**: a feed of trending papers, comments under each paper, an AI overview and AI tools on top.
- **SummarizePaper is a summary you can read in two minutes**: a detailed summary, key points, a lay summary, related papers and the BibTeX entry on one public page, in English or French, plus a daily email digest.
- **Use alphaXiv** to see what the community says about a paper. **Use SummarizePaper** to decide quickly whether to read it, or to understand it outside your field.
- Both work from the arXiv ID, so nothing stops you from using both on the same paper.

## alphaXiv vs SummarizePaper at a glance

| | alphaXiv | SummarizePaper |
|---|---|---|
| Main idea | Discussion and AI layer on top of arXiv | Written AI summary page for each arXiv paper |
| How to open a paper | Replace `arxiv` with `alphaxiv` in the URL | arXiv ID or search on the home page |
| What you get on a paper | Abstract, AI overview, comments, audio | Detailed summary, key points, lay summary, blog-style article, related papers, BibTeX, PDF export |
| Ask questions about a paper | Yes | Yes, from the full text, on one or several papers |
| Languages | Several languages for AI overviews | English and French |
| Following new papers | Trending feed, researchers to follow | Daily email digest by arXiv category |
| Community | Comments and discussion | None |
| Price | No pricing listed | Free; Premium €7/month |

## What alphaXiv does better

- **Discussion.** Each paper has a discussion space, so you can see questions and remarks from other readers. SummarizePaper has nothing comparable.
- **Discovery.** The home page ranks trending papers with a one-sentence takeaway each, and lets you follow researchers. When we checked in October 2026, the trending papers were mostly machine learning.
- **Tooling for developers.** alphaXiv offers a browser extension and an MCP server to plug papers into AI assistants.

## What SummarizePaper does better

- **A complete summary on one page.** The detailed summary, key points and a lay summary anyone can understand are written in advance, so the page loads ready to read. See [an example](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/paper/2302.13303v1/).
- **Reading outside your field.** The lay summary and blog-style article explain the paper without jargon.
- **French.** Every summary exists in English or French.
- **A digest that comes to you.** The [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) emails up to 10 new papers every weekday from up to 5 arXiv categories you choose, with optional keywords, each with a two-sentence summary.
- **Citing and exporting.** Each page gives the BibTeX entry and a PDF of the summary.

| Understand a paper outside your field | SummarizePaper (lay summary) |
| See what other researchers think of a paper | alphaXiv |
| Browse what is trending in machine learning this week | alphaXiv |
| Get new papers in your categories by email every morning | SummarizePaper |
| Read a summary in French | SummarizePaper |

## Who SummarizePaper is not for

If what you value is the conversation around a paper, or a social feed of trending work, alphaXiv is the better home. SummarizePaper has no comments and no social features: it is built for reading and understanding papers fast.
