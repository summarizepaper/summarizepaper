{% trans 'How it works' %}

  1. {% trans 'Every week, we read the title and abstract of every new paper on arXiv (first versions only, not updates) and note the terms of one to three words they use.' %}
  2. {% trans 'For your term, we count the papers using it each week, and divide by all the papers of that week. A share rather than a number: arXiv grows every year, so the number of papers would rise for almost every topic, while the share rises only when the topic takes a larger place.' %}
  3. {% blocktrans with weeks=recent_weeks %}The answer at the top compares the last {{ weeks }} weeks with the {{ weeks }} weeks before. The topic "rises" only when its share is at least ×1.2 and the gap is too large to come from chance, a test of the same kind as for the rising topics of the week; it "declines" in the same way the other way round.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans 'How to read it' %}

  • {% trans 'A curve that climbs over several months: more and more teams write about the topic. A peak that falls back after a few weeks: an event, a release, a data set or a conference deadline.' %}
  • {% trans 'The height matters less than the trend: a broad term (graph neural network) is always higher than a precise one (equivariant message passing). To compare two topics, compare how their curves move.' %}
  • {% trans 'The terms are counted as words, in English, plurals with their singular. A term with several meanings counts them all (transformer, in electrical engineering and in AI): then try a more specific term. A week where a single paper uses the term counts as zero.' %}
  • {% trans 'The curve says how much the topic is written about, not whether the papers are good or cited, nor what is published outside arXiv.' %}

{% trans 'Why it is useful' %}

  • {% trans 'Check an impression: "everyone is working on it" — true, or only in the papers you happen to see?' %}
  • {% trans 'Choose a thesis or project subject: a rising topic draws funding and competition; a declining one may be maturing, or being left aside.' %}
  • {% trans 'Back up a "growing interest" in a grant proposal or an introduction with a number. When citing it, name the source and the period.' %}
