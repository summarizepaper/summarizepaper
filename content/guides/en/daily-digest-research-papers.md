Build a morning reading queue you can actually finish. This example follows planetary astrophysics and shows how to inspect the selection before changing your settings.

## A real example

This live preview is configured in English and contains 20 papers from the announcement. Open papers that address your question and inspect why they were selected. ## Reproduce the workflow

1. Choose your categories, summary language, reading volume and delivery time zone.

2. With Premium, describe the question you study or add representative papers. Inspect the live preview before saving.

3. Open a useful paper from an edition, check why it was selected and save it to a collection. Adjust one setting if the selection is too broad.

## Turn the result into a decision

Use the digest for a recurring selection across your interests. For one exact query, choose Saved searches; for a narrated explanation of a neighboring field, choose Weekly letters. A good selection produces a short reading queue, not just another email to archive.

## Which research watch fits your need?

| Your need | Tool | What you follow |
|---|---|---|
| Build a short daily reading queue | Daily digest | Your fields and interests |
| Understand a neighboring field each week | [Weekly letters](../weekly-science-letters/) | An editorial account of publications |
| Track an expression or query | [Saved searches](../saved-searches-research-alerts/) | New matching results |
| Spot work close to a project | [Scoop watch](../scoop-watch-research-project/) · Premium | Question, method and contribution |
| Understand who builds on a paper | [Citation alerts](../track-research-paper-citations/) · Premium | Citations and citing passages |
