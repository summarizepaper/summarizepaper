Key takeaways

- **ChatPDF** is a chat for any PDF: upload a document, ask questions, get answers. Free for 2 documents a day.
- **SummarizePaper** is built for arXiv papers: type the arXiv ID and get a full summary page (detailed summary, key points, lay summary, related papers, BibTeX), plus an assistant for questions.
- **Use ChatPDF** for journal articles, reports and anything that is not on arXiv. **Use SummarizePaper** for arXiv papers and to follow new ones by email.

</div>

<p class="note">Disclosure: this comparison is written by the creator of SummarizePaper. ChatPDF features were checked on its site on 18 October 2026.</p>

## ChatPDF vs SummarizePaper at a glance

| | ChatPDF | SummarizePaper |
|---|---|---|
| Works with | Any PDF you upload | Papers on arXiv, from the arXiv ID |
| What you get | Answers in a chat | A summary page: detailed summary, key points, lay summary, blog-style article, related papers, BibTeX, PDF export |
| Questions on the paper | Yes | Yes, from the full text |
| Several papers at once | Yes, multi-file chats | Yes, on the chat page |
| Account needed | No | No to read; free account for the digest and more questions |
| Free limits | 2 documents a day | Unlimited reading, 10 new summaries and 30 questions a day without an account |
| Paid plan | ChatPDF Plus, unlimited documents | Premium €7/month |
| Summary languages | Not checked | English and French |
| New papers by email | No | Daily digest by arXiv category |

## What ChatPDF does better

- **Any document.** Journal articles behind a publisher's site, theses, reports, books: if you have the PDF, ChatPDF reads it. SummarizePaper only covers arXiv.
- **Free-form questions.** You steer the conversation from the first message, which suits a precise question such as "what sample size did they use?".
- **More than papers.** ChatPDF also offers chat on YouTube videos and writing tools.

## What SummarizePaper does better

- **No download, no upload.** Type the arXiv ID or search the home page; if someone already summarized the paper, the [summary page](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/arxiv-id/2302.13303v1/) opens immediately.
- **A summary you do not have to ask for.** The page is structured the same way for every paper, including a lay summary anyone can understand.
- **Something you can keep and share.** Each summary is a public page with the BibTeX entry and a PDF export, not a chat that disappears in your history.
- **Following the field.** The [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) emails up to 10 new papers every weekday from the arXiv categories you choose.

## Which one should you use?

| Your situation | Use |
|---|---|
| The paper is on arXiv | SummarizePaper |
| The paper is a journal PDF or not public | ChatPDF |
| You have one precise question about a document | ChatPDF, or the SummarizePaper assistant for arXiv papers |
| You want to decide quickly whether to read a paper | SummarizePaper |
| You want new papers in your field every morning | SummarizePaper |

## Who SummarizePaper is not for

If most of what you read is not on arXiv, ChatPDF or another PDF tool will serve you better: SummarizePaper cannot read files you upload.
