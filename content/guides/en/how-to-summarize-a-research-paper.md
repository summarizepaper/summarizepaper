## Reproduce the workflow

1. Open 1703.10693v1 on SummarizePaper and check the source-scope statement.

2. Read the question, method, findings and limitations before opening the longer summary.

3. Choose one claim that matters to your work and locate it in the original abstract or PDF.

## Turn the result into a decision

Finish with a reading decision: methods to inspect now, a paper to keep for later, or a reference to leave aside. Changing the explanation level helps with vocabulary; it does not add source material.
