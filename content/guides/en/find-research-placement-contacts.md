Find people whose recent research connects to what you want to learn. The goal is a well-informed enquiry, with papers to read before making contact.

## A real example

We ran a fictional Bachelor-level search with Python and numerical-modelling skills, a six-month duration and no geographical restriction. The results group potential contacts with publication evidence and acknowledged funding. Both runs explicitly report incomplete coverage and people whose institutions could not be located. These are leads to verify, not vacancies or confirmed supervisors. No enquiry was sent.

## Reproduce the workflow

1. Enter your level, research topic, skills, preferred location and timing in the Premium+ placement tool. Our fictional profile is L3/Bachelor, Python and numerical modelling, six months, with no location restriction.

2. Read the publication evidence and suggested readings for one lead. Check the institution’s current pages and the application procedure.

3. Save a shortlist note explaining the scientific fit and the unresolved questions about supervision, dates and funding. Prepare a personal enquiry only after reading a relevant paper.

## Turn the result into a decision

A publication-based lead is not a vacancy. Historical affiliations and funding acknowledgements do not establish current availability or an internship budget. Before sending a message, confirm those points with institutional sources. This demonstration sent no enquiries.
