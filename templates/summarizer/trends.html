{% trans 'How it works' %}

{% trans 'We count arXiv, bioRxiv and medRxiv separately: each server, and each of their fields, has its own rising topics. Journal articles are not counted.' %}

{% trans 'Each week, we count how many papers submitted that week (first versions) use each term of one to three words in their title and abstract; acronyms such as RAG or JWST count, and plurals with their singular. A term rises when its share of papers is at least twice its share over the eight weeks before, when so large a rise is very unlikely by chance (a log-likelihood ratio of at least 15), and when it comes from at least three teams, none signing more than half of the papers: one group posting a series is not a trend. An AI then groups the rising terms into topics and names them; it never decides what rises: the counts do.' %}

  • {% trans 'New' %}: {% trans 'the term was almost absent from these papers before.' %}
  • {% trans 'Rising for several weeks' %}: {% trans 'up three weeks in a row or more, not a one-week event.' %}
  • {% trans 'Recurring' %}: {% trans 'as frequent at this time last year: perhaps a yearly cycle, such as a conference deadline.' %}
  • {% trans 'Comeback' %}: {% trans 'as frequent some months ago, then less used.' %}
  • {% trans 'Sudden rise' %}: {% trans 'new this week: often an event, a release, a data set or a deadline.' %}

{% trans 'The curve on each topic is the share of papers using its main term, per 10,000 papers, over the last six months. Counting the previous Monday-to-Sunday submission week starts on Tuesday at 06:00 UTC, after the normal Monday arXiv announcement. Delayed announcements may still be missing from the first count.' %}
