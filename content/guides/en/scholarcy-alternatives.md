Key takeaways

- **For arXiv and journal papers**: SummarizePaper (free, works from the arXiv ID or the DOI, with answers you can check against the paper) or alphaXiv (arXiv, summary plus discussion).
- **For any PDF**: ChatPDF (free for 2 documents a day) or SciSummary (Pro plan at $48 a year).
- **For a literature review**: Elicit for a systematic review; SummarizePaper Premium+ for a first map of the literature on a research question.
- **For questions across your own papers**: NotebookLM, free with up to 50 sources per notebook.
- **Keep Scholarcy** if its flashcards fit the way you study: none of these alternatives copies that format.

## Why look for a Scholarcy alternative?

[Scholarcy](https://www.scholarcy.com/) turns an article into flashcard-style summaries with background, methods and findings. The free summarizer stops after 10 summaries, and the monthly plan costs €8.99 per month after a one-week trial; it adds unlimited and enhanced summaries, flashcard collections, a literature matrix and one-click bibliographies. People usually look elsewhere for one of four reasons: the free limit, they mostly read arXiv preprints, they need a tool that finds papers, not only summarizes them, or they want to ask questions and see where the answers come from rather than read a fixed summary.

## The alternatives, one by one

### SummarizePaper

[SummarizePaper](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/) publishes an AI summary page for papers from arXiv, journals (from their DOI), bioRxiv, medRxiv, HAL and other open repositories, generated from the full text when the license allows it: a detailed summary, key points, a lay summary, a blog-style article, related papers, the BibTeX entry and a PDF export. Its [AI assistant](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/chat/) answers questions on one or several papers and lists, after each answer, the sentence of the paper that supports each claim, checked word for word, with a link to the passage. A free [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) emails new arXiv, bioRxiv and medRxiv papers in your categories. Premium adds collections with comparison tables, a bibliography check and "What did I miss?" for your draft, citation alerts and a scoop watch; Premium+ adds an exploratory literature review from a research question, journal club slides and press kits.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** preprints and papers you can name by DOI, checking AI answers against the paper, summaries in French and rewrites in 16 languages, following new papers, preparing a submission.
- **Worse for:** PDFs with no public record (there is no upload to summarize) and flashcards.

### alphaXiv

[alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/) opens any arXiv paper with an AI overview, comments from other readers, an audio summary and AI chat. Replace `arxiv` with `alphaxiv` in a paper's URL to open it there.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** seeing what other researchers say about a paper.
- **Worse for:** papers outside arXiv.

In detail: [alphaXiv vs SummarizePaper](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/guides/alphaxiv-vs-summarizepaper/).

### Elicit

[Elicit](https://elicit.com/) searches more than 138 million papers and extracts findings into tables. The free Basic plan includes unlimited summaries and chat with papers; Pro costs $49 per month.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** systematic reviews, because it finds the papers for you; Scholarcy's literature matrix only compares papers you import.
- **Worse for:** a quick read of a single paper.

### NotebookLM

Google's [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) answers only from the sources you add, with citations. The free plan allows 100 notebooks of up to 50 sources each.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** asking questions across a reading list.
- **Worse for:** a ready-made structured summary; you have to ask.

### ChatPDF

[ChatPDF](https://www.chatpdf.com/) summarizes and answers questions on any PDF, without signing up. The free plan covers 2 documents a day; ChatPDF Plus removes that limit. It also lets you chat with several PDFs at once.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** any PDF, quick questions.
- **Worse for:** a structured summary you can keep; the answer lives in a chat.

In detail: [ChatPDF vs SummarizePaper](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/guides/chatpdf-vs-summarizepaper/).

### SciSummary

[SciSummary](https://scisummary.com/) summarizes uploaded papers and analyzes their figures. After a 7-day trial, the Pro plan costs $48 per year.

- **Better than Scholarcy for:** price if you summarize papers all year ($48 a year against €8.99 a month).

### SciSpace

[SciSpace](https://scispace.com/) combines paper search, chat with PDFs and explanations while you read. Check [its pricing page](https://scispace.com/pricing) for current plans.

## How to choose in one question

Where do your papers come from? If they are on arXiv or have a DOI, start with SummarizePaper (or alphaXiv for arXiv discussion). If you only have the PDFs, start with ChatPDF or SciSummary. If it is "hundreds of them" for a systematic review, go to Elicit.

For a wider comparison, see [Best AI tools to summarize arXiv papers](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/guides/best-ai-tools-to-summarize-arxiv-papers/).
