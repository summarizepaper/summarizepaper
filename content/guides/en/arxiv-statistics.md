Key numbers

- **3,215,245** submissions on arXiv as of 18 October 2026.
- **284,486** new submissions in 2025, **+16.6%** on 2024, about **780 a day**.
- **+30.0%**: submissions from January to September 2026 compared with the same months of 2025.
- **40,363** submissions in September 2026, the first month above 40,000.
- **45.3%** of 2025 submissions were in computer science, up from 34.8% in 2022.
- **cs.AI** submissions rose **150%** from January–September 2025 to January–September 2026.

<p class="note">All figures come from arXiv's own statistics, downloaded on 18 October 2026 and compiled by SummarizePaper. Category figures cover January 2022 to September 2026.</p>

## How many papers are on arXiv?

arXiv had received **3,215,245 submissions** as of 18 October 2026, with statistics running since August 1991 (arXiv, Monthly Submissions, 2026).

It took arXiv until **2015** to reach 100,000 submissions in a single year (105,280). Ten years later, in 2025, it received 284,486.

- **arXiv received 284,486 new submissions in 2025**, an average of 23,707 a month and about 780 a day.
- **Growth slowed to about 2% a year in 2021 and 2022**, then picked up: +12.3% in 2023, +17.0% in 2024 and +16.6% in 2025.
- **Submissions more than doubled between 2017 (123,523) and 2025 (284,486).**

## Is arXiv growth accelerating in 2026?

Yes. From January to September 2026, arXiv received **270,685 submissions**, **30.0% more** than the 208,241 of January to September 2025 (arXiv monthly submissions, 2026).

- **September 2026 set the monthly record with 40,363 submissions**, the first month above 40,000. The previous high was 32,040 in June 2026.
- **By 18 October 2026, the year's count (293,057) had already passed the full 2025 total (284,486).**

## Which fields publish the most on arXiv?

arXiv groups its categories into eight subject groups. In 2025 (arXiv, Submissions by Category, 2026):

| Group | Submissions in 2025 | Share | Share Jan–Sep 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Computer science (cs) | 128,113 | 45.3% | 47.6% |
| Physics (all archives) | 85,121 | 30.1% | 27.4% |
| Mathematics (math) | 43,730 | 15.4% | 16.9% |
| Electrical engineering and systems science (eess) | 12,542 | 4.4% | 3.7% |
| Statistics (stat) | 6,916 | 2.4% | 2.5% |
| Quantitative biology (q-bio) | 3,214 | 1.1% | 0.8% |
| Economics (econ) | 2,288 | 0.8% | 0.8% |
| Quantitative finance (q-fin) | 1,196 | 0.4% | 0.4% |

Within physics, 2025 submissions split into other physics archives (36,435: quant-ph, gr-qc, nuclear physics, nonlinear sciences and the physics.* categories), condensed matter (18,679), astrophysics (18,635) and high-energy physics (11,372).

## Is computer science taking over arXiv?

Its share keeps growing. Computer science made up **34.8%** of arXiv submissions in 2022, **38.7%** in 2023, **42.7%** in 2024, **45.3%** in 2025 and **47.6%** from January to September 2026 (arXiv, Submissions by Category, 2026).

Other fields are not shrinking: physics submissions grew 20% and mathematics 44% from January–September 2025 to January–September 2026. Computer science simply grows faster (+36%).

## How to keep up with this volume

At about 780 new papers a day in 2025, and more in 2026, nobody reads everything in even one large category: cs.LG alone received about 70 papers a day in 2025. Two habits help:

- **Filter by category and keyword** instead of reading full listings. SummarizePaper's free [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) sends up to 10 new papers every weekday from up to 5 arXiv, bioRxiv or medRxiv categories you choose, each with a two-sentence summary; with Premium, up to 25 papers ranked by closeness to your own research.
- **Read a summary before the paper.** [Look up any arXiv paper](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/), or a journal article from its DOI, for a detailed summary, key points and a lay summary, or see [which AI tools summarize arXiv papers](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/guides/best-ai-tools-to-summarize-arxiv-papers/).

## Methodology and limits

- **Yearly totals** are the sum of the monthly counts in arXiv's monthly submissions CSV for each calendar year, downloaded on 18 October 2026. The 2026 figure is partial (to 18 October).
- **Category figures** come from the data behind arXiv's "Submissions by Category since 2022" charts, which count each submission once under its primary category. They cover January 2022 to September 2026. Their 2025 total (283,120) is within 0.5% of the monthly count (284,486); arXiv publishes the two datasets separately and they differ slightly.
- **Physics** follows arXiv's own grouping: astrophysics (astro-ph), condensed matter (cond-mat), high-energy physics (hep-ph, hep-th, hep-ex, hep-lat) and the other physics archives (quant-ph, gr-qc, nucl-ex, nucl-th, nlin and physics.*).
- Submissions are new papers, not new versions of existing papers.
- This page is updated when arXiv publishes new figures. Last update: 18 October 2026.
