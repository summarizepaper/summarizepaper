{% trans 'How it works' %}

{% trans 'Each week, we count how many papers submitted that week (first versions) use each term of one to three words in their title and abstract; acronyms such as RAG or JWST count, and plurals with their singular. A term rises when its share of papers is at least twice its share over the eight weeks before, when so large a rise is very unlikely by chance (a log-likelihood ratio of at least 15), and when it comes from at least three teams, none signing more than half of the papers: one group posting a series is not a trend. An AI then groups the rising terms into topics and names them; it never decides what rises: the counts do.' %}
