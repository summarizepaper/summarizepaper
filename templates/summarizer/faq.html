{% trans "Frequently Asked Questions" %}

{% trans "What is this website about?" %}
{% trans "SummarizePaper summarizes arXiv papers with AI: for each paper you get a detailed summary, the key points, a lay summary anyone can understand and a blog-style article, in English or French, as well as a list of related papers. An AI assistant answers your questions about one or several papers, and a free daily email digest brings you the new papers in your fields." %}
{% trans "How does the AI summarization work?" %}
{% trans "We read the full text of the paper (the HTML version published by arXiv when it exists, the PDF otherwise) and send it to recent OpenAI language models with a series of prompts that produce each part of the summary. Free users get our standard model; Premium subscribers get our most capable model, for summaries as well as for answers." %}
{% trans "How do I get the summary of a paper?" %}
{% trans "Search for a paper with the search bar on the home page, or type its arXiv ID. If the paper has already been summarized, the summary shows up immediately. If it is the first time someone looks for it, our AI has to read and summarize it first, which can take a few minutes; the progress is shown on the page." %}
{% trans "Does the AI assistant read the whole paper?" %}
{% blocktrans %}Yes. The assistant answers from the full text of the paper, not just the abstract, and its answers appear as they are written. Ask it questions from the paper page, or on the chat page to question several papers at once.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "Is this website free to use?" %}
{% blocktrans %}Yes. Reading summaries, asking questions and receiving the daily digest are free, within the daily limits below. An optional Premium subscription gives you our most capable model and higher limits.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "Is there a limit to the number of papers I can summarize?" %}

{% blocktrans %}Reading papers that are already summarized is unlimited. Starting new summaries is limited to {{ runs_per_day }} per day for free users and unlimited with Premium.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Questions to the AI assistant are limited to {{ chat_anonymous }} per day without an account, {{ chat_free }} per day with a free account and {{ chat_paid }} per day with Premium.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What does Premium include and how do I cancel?" %}
{% blocktrans %}Premium gives you our most capable AI model for summaries and answers, no daily summary limit and {{ chat_paid }} questions per day to the assistant. It is billed monthly or yearly through Stripe; we never see your card details. You can cancel in one click with the "Manage my subscription" button on the Premium page: Premium then stays active until the end of the period you paid for.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "How does the daily digest work?" %}
{% blocktrans %}On the digest page, choose up to {{ max_categories }} arXiv categories, optional keywords, the language of the summaries and the time you want to receive it. Every weekday, you get up to {{ papers_per_email }} new papers, each with a 2-sentence plain-language summary and a link to the full AI summary. No digest is sent on Saturday and Sunday in your time zone. You can also receive it once a week instead. Every email has a link to unsubscribe in one click.{% endblocktrans %} {% blocktrans %}With Premium, the digest ranks papers by closeness to a description of your research or to papers you choose, follows authors across all categories, adds a synthesis of the week in your fields, and has the full summary of your best papers ready before you open the email. You can have up to 5 digests, with 15 categories and 25 papers each.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "Do I need an account?" %}
{% blocktrans %}No account is needed to read summaries or ask a few questions. A free account is needed for the daily digest, for Premium and for more questions per day. You can sign in with Google, or receive a login link by email: no password to remember.{% endblocktrans %}
{% trans "Can I export a summary or a reference?" %}
{% trans "Yes. Each paper page lets you download the summary as a PDF and get the BibTeX entry of the paper for your bibliography." %}
