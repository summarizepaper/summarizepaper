{% trans "What we collect and why" %}

{% trans 'Your account' %}

{% trans 'Your username, email address and password (stored only as a hash), or, if you sign in with Google, your name and email address as Google gives them. We use them to sign you in and to send you the emails you asked for. Legal basis: providing the service you signed up for.' %}

{% trans 'What you do with your account' %}

{% trans 'The papers you read, your chat questions and their answers, your collections, saved searches, digests and alerts and their settings, the papers and authors you follow, the projects you describe for the scoop watch, your placement shortlists and the results you generate (press kits, literature reviews, slides...). {% trans 'Without an account' %}

{% trans 'When you request a summary, ask a question or vote without an account, we keep the request with a hashed form of your IP address (never the address itself), to apply the daily limits and let you continue your conversation. {% trans 'Files and texts you submit' %}

{% trans 'The drafts, bibliographies and conference programs you upload or paste are read to produce the result you asked for and are not kept on the site; only that result is saved in your account.' %}

{% trans 'Emails' %}

{% trans 'For the emails we send, our email provider tells us whether they were delivered or rejected, and we stop writing to addresses that bounce or unsubscribe. In the daily digest, we record which papers you open, with their rank in the email, to improve the ranking; these statistics are deleted after 90 days. We may occasionally write to account holders to ask for their feedback; each of these emails has a link to stop them.' %}

{% trans 'Payments' %}

{% trans 'Premium payments are handled by Stripe: we never see or store your card number. We keep your plan, its dates and your Stripe customer reference, and the invoices are kept as long as the law requires.' %}

{% trans 'Contact form' %}

{% trans 'Your name, email address and message are sent to us by email so that we can answer you; they are not stored on the site. A fingerprint of the message is kept for 24 hours to avoid duplicates.' %}

{% trans 'Shared links' %}

{% trans 'When you share a conversation, a result or a collection, anyone with the link can read it until the link expires or you stop sharing; members of a shared collection see its papers and notes.' %}

{% trans "Artificial intelligence" %}

{% trans 'Summaries, answers and other results are written with the OpenAI API. To produce them, we send OpenAI the text of the papers, your questions and the texts you submit, never your email address. According to its terms for API customers, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models and may keep it up to 30 days to detect abuse.' %}

{% trans 'We only use cookies needed for the site to work: one to keep you signed in, and one to protect forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks. As these are strictly necessary, no consent banner is needed.' %}

{% trans 'We measure our audience with Umami, which sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses. Sign-in links and tokens are removed from the addresses it receives, and we only see aggregate figures such as page views, referring sites, countries and the steps of sign-up and subscription.' %}
