## A real example

We saved the exact query and ran its two-week preview. It returned zero new papers. The general search results and the watch preview are different: related search results do not prove an exact watch match. The example remains saved with its watch disabled; no new email watch was activated.

## Reproduce the workflow

1. Search for `"debris discs" gas` and inspect the sources and filters before saving.

2. Save the search with an account and open its two-week preview. Compare the matching rules with the general search results.

3. If the preview is too narrow, try fewer terms or alternatives with OR and compare again. Enable delivery only when the scope is useful; web and RSS options are available too.

## Turn the result into a decision

An empty preview does not prove that the literature is empty. Check vocabulary and source-availability notes. Categories can exclude journal searches, and year filters shape the search page rather than restricting the watch to old years. Use Scoop Watch for project meaning across different wording.
