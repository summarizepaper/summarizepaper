Key takeaways

- **ChatPDF** is a chat for any PDF: upload a document, ask questions, get answers. Free for 2 documents a day.
- **SummarizePaper** is built for research papers that have a public record: arXiv, journal articles from their DOI, bioRxiv, medRxiv, HAL and more. You get a full summary page, and an assistant that shows the sentence of the paper behind each claim it makes.
- **Use ChatPDF** for reports, theses, books and PDFs with no DOI. **Use SummarizePaper** for published papers and preprints, to check AI answers against the paper, and to follow new papers by email.

## ChatPDF vs SummarizePaper at a glance

| | ChatPDF | SummarizePaper |
|---|---|---|
| Works with | Any PDF you upload | Papers with a public record: arXiv, DOI, bioRxiv, medRxiv, HAL, Europe PMC, OSF preprints |
| What you get | Answers in a chat | A summary page: detailed summary, key points, lay summary, structured reading, blog-style article, related papers, BibTeX, PDF export |
| Questions on the paper | Yes | Yes, from the full text and its figures |
| Where each claim comes from | Not checked | Each claim with the paper's sentence, checked word for word, and a link to the passage |
| Several papers at once | Yes, multi-file chats | Yes, on the chat page and in collections with comparison tables |
| Account needed | No | No to read; free account for the digest and more questions |
| Free limits | 2 documents a day | Unlimited reading, 10 new summaries and 30 questions a day without an account |
| Paid plan | ChatPDF Plus, unlimited documents | Premium €7/month, Premium+ €14/month |
| Summary languages | Not checked | English and French; rewrites in 16 languages |
| New papers by email | No | Daily digest of arXiv, bioRxiv and medRxiv papers |

## What ChatPDF does better

- **Any document.** Theses, reports, books, internal documents: if you have the PDF, ChatPDF reads it. SummarizePaper needs a paper it can find from an identifier.
- **Free-form questions.** You steer the conversation from the first message, which suits a precise question such as "what sample size did they use?".
- **More than papers.** ChatPDF also offers chat on YouTube videos and writing tools.

## What SummarizePaper does better

- **No download, no upload.** Type an arXiv ID, paste a DOI or a link, or search the home page across arXiv and open-access journals; if someone already summarized the paper, the [summary page](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/paper/2302.13303v1/) opens immediately.
- **Answers you can check.** After each answer, the [assistant](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/chat/) lists its claims, each with the sentence of the paper that supports it, the section or figure it comes from and a link to that passage. Claims the paper does not support are labelled.
- **A summary you do not have to ask for.** The page is structured the same way for every paper, including a lay summary anyone can understand and rewrites for other reading levels and languages.
- **Something you can keep and share.** Each summary is a public page with the BibTeX entry, a PDF export and exports to Zotero, Notion and Obsidian, not a chat that disappears in your history.
- **Following the field.** The [daily digest](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/digest/) emails new papers from the categories you choose; with Premium, citation and new-version alerts on the papers you follow.
- **Your own draft, without summarizing it.** With Premium, upload your draft to [find the close papers it does not cite](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/missed/) or to [check its bibliography](https://www.summarizepaper.com/en/refcheck/) for retracted papers and citations the cited abstract does not support.

## Which one should you use?

| Your situation | Use |
|---|---|
| The paper is on arXiv or has a DOI | SummarizePaper |
| The document is a report, a thesis or has no public record | ChatPDF |
| You need to know which passage supports an answer | SummarizePaper |
| You have one precise question about any document | ChatPDF, or the SummarizePaper assistant for papers it knows |
| You want to decide quickly whether to read a paper | SummarizePaper |
| You want new papers in your field every morning | SummarizePaper |

## Who SummarizePaper is not for

If most of what you read has no DOI or public record, ChatPDF or another PDF tool will serve you better: SummarizePaper does not summarize PDFs you upload.
