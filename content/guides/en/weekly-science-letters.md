Discover a neighboring field without beginning with a long list of abstracts. A weekly letter explains a selection of papers and gives you sources to follow when one result catches your attention.

## Reproduce the workflow

1. Choose a theme and open a dated edition. The example is Astronomy and space, 26 October 2026.

2. Read the narrative once, then choose one result and open its source paper. Check the study type, publication status and main limitation.

3. Keep one useful reading lead or take a focused question into the chat. Follow the theme on the web, by RSS or through the email options available to your account.

## Turn the result into a decision

The useful outcome is one new scientific connection. A free account can receive one selected letter; Premium provides multiple chosen letters and additional listening and personalization options. Use Daily digest for a recurring paper queue and the letters for an editorial explanation of a field.
