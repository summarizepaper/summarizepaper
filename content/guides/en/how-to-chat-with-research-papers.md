Ask a question that a summary alone cannot answer. In this example, three papers are selected to separate a general model, an observational search and a model of one system.

## A real example

We also opened the three papers actually used in the tutorial collection (1703.10693v1, 2110.02308v1 and 1606.01247v1). We asked the assistant to distinguish a general model, an observational search and a model of one particular system. The conversation was executed separately in each language. All three selected sources were marked abstract-only under their licenses. This is a real multi-paper answer with source checks, not a comparison of full texts.

## Reproduce the workflow

1. Add 1703.10693v1, 2110.02308v1 and 1606.01247v1 on the chat page. Wait until each source has loaded.

2. Ask which paper presents a general model, which an observational search and which a model of one specific system. Request sources and reading limitations.

3. Open one source check and compare the answer with its passage. Follow up on an assumption, rather than asking for another generic summary.

## Turn the result into a decision

The useful outcome is a passage to read or an assumption to test. Source-check flags need interpretation: a statement about the assistant’s own access may be true without appearing in the scientific paper.
