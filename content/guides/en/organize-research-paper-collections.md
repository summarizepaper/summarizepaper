Turn a reading list into a comparison you can inspect. Our tutorial collection combines a general gas model, an ALMA search and a model of β Pictoris.

## A real example

The collection contains 1703.10693v1, 2110.02308v1 and 1606.01247v1. Its comparison table has 13 filled cells, each supported by source passages. The available sources here are abstracts and summaries; the full-text sections were not read. Open the associated passages to inspect what supports each comparison.

## Reproduce the workflow

1. Create a collection for a question: “How can observations distinguish the origin of gas in debris discs?”

2. Add 1703.10693v1, 2110.02308v1 and 1606.01247v1; keep a note on the role of each paper.

3. With Premium, select the papers and generate the comparison table. Inspect a supporting passage and keep “not stated” where information is missing.

## Turn the result into a decision

A difference between a population model and one observed system is not automatically a contradiction. Use the table to choose comparable quantities, then export the references or continue in the chat. Basic collections are available with an account; source-backed comparison is Premium.
